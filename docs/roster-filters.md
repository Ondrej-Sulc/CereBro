# Champion and roster filters

The compact tray refinement of the quick-bar layout (prototype A) was selected on 2026-10-23. On desktop, rarity, named classes, and the compact ownership dropdown stay visible. Active rules sit directly below these quick choices, above the expandable tray. The tray contains multi-select rank and ascension dropdowns, four aligned pickers (tags, ability categories, abilities, protection), and source-refinement popovers. There are no repeated section headings or empty columns. Sorting, **Filters**, the roster actions menu, and Add sit beside search. Edit and attack reservation actions retain their independent permissions; active modes have visible exit controls.

Below 640px, the collapsed toolbar uses three compact rows: search with the roster actions menu; rarity with a multi-select class popover and filter trigger; then ownership and sorting. The class popover keeps full names, multiple selections, a clear action, and a Done button. **Filters** opens a modal bottom sheet with Roster, Tags, Abilities, and Protection tabs. Its body scrolls while Clear all and **Show N champions** remain visible above the safe area. Every filter remains available, including multiple ranks and ascension level 0. The sheet traps focus, locks background scrolling, closes with Escape or the overlay, and returns focus to the filter trigger. Nested pickers close before the sheet on Escape. Filtering remains immediate and selections persist across tabs and closing. Search uses 16px text to avoid focus zoom on mobile browsers.

Pickers inside the mobile sheet open their own modal dialog and own the active focus and scroll lock. They center within `visualViewport` and follow its height and offset as the keyboard opens, instead of squeezing the list into the space beside its trigger. The option list scrolls while the page stays locked. Closing the picker restores the sheet's lock and returns focus to its trigger; closing the sheet releases the final lock. Desktop pickers retain their anchored, nonmodal popovers. Browser regression checks must scroll an overflowing option list with real input, verify that the list moves while the page stays locked, and check Done/Escape focus restoration. At a 390×430 reduced viewport, the list must retain room for several options (the old anchored picker collapsed to 3px; the viewport-sized picker retains 181px). Setting `scrollTop` alone bypasses scroll locking and cannot catch this regression.

The visual polish uses quiet, borderless inactive choices and a restrained accent for selections. Active selections are grouped into complete rules, for example **Bleed protection · ≥80% potency** or **Bleed or Shock · Debuff · Inflicts**. Removing a rule clears all of its qualifiers; other rules remain active. Long summaries wrap on narrow screens. The potency editor offers **Any**, **50%**, **80%**, and **100%** presets alongside custom numeric entry, with the resistance switch and minimum inside **Refine immunity sources**.

Immunities and resistances are included by default. **Refine immunity sources** contains **Include resistances** and an optional **Minimum potency reduction** percentage. Turning resistances off narrows matching to immunities and temporarily disables the saved minimum. **Clear all** restores the inclusive default and clears the minimum and ability refinements, while leaving sorting and roster management modes alone.

Ability names, forms, and directions must match the same source application. For protections, an 80% minimum accepts full immunities and resistance applications with a recorded potency (or combined potency/duration) reduction of at least 80%. Unknown, duration-only, damage-only, and unspecified resistance values cannot establish a potency minimum. Separate applications are not added together. All/any effect matching runs after qualification. A minimum without selected effects requires some qualifying protection. Recorded rarity restrictions apply to both groups.

Champion quick views highlight qualifying sources and show their matching conditions, including when multiple percentages share a source group. Unknown ability form/direction metadata remains searchable as **Not recorded**.

While quick-view sources load, the kit overview announces loading once and effect tiles stay static. A loading placeholder never creates an expandable panel. Once sources arrive, matched effects with usable conditions can auto-expand; source-less effects remain static throughout, without briefly opening and closing.

Ability and protection picker rows include the effect's recorded icon and glossary colour when available. Effects without icons keep the same text alignment. Icon metadata is shared by effect name across the catalog; resistance options use their target effect's icon, never the source ability's icon. Tags and category pickers remain text-based.

Filter options update contextually as search and other selections change. Pickers keep available options alphabetical with a count of matching roster entries; zero-match choices are behind **Show unavailable** and disabled. Selected choices always remain visible and removable, including at zero matches or when resistances are turned off. The quick rarity/class row stays compact, dimming unavailable choices and exposing counts on hover; class/progression popovers and the mobile sheet show counts directly.

An **Any selected** facet ignores its own current choices when calculating each option's count, so choosing Science still allows adding Cosmic. An **All selected** facet retains its current choices and counts champions that also match the candidate. Counts represent that option's matching slice, not the combined total after adding an OR choice. Other filters remain active, including search, ownership, category/effect relationships, same-application form/direction qualifiers, rarity restrictions and the minimum potency. A champion is counted once per roster entry, regardless of repeated sources; separate owned rarities count as separate entries, consistent with the results. Rarity alternatives also recalculate missing owned rarities and obtainability for unowned cards. Counts and results share the same matching functions, and parsed source data is reused across changes; sorting does not recalculate facets.

The alliance roster overview reuses the same toolbar, active rules, mobile sheet, effect pickers and champion quick view. Battlegroup (including Unassigned) and the per-player display cap are available beside search on desktop and in the mobile Roster tab. Battlegroup scopes both results and contextual counts. Counts include all matching roster entries before the Top 5/10/20 cap; the same champion on different players counts separately. The result summary and each player's row distinguish displayed champions from total matches. Clear all resets battlegroup and champion criteria while retaining the display cap. Alliance rosters stay owned-only and keep their strongest-first ordering, tactic indicators and member grouping. On mobile, member headings sit above their champions. Source metadata and validated resistance targets use the shared roster shape; repeated champion/rarity kits are prepared once across members.
