2026-11-04. Second candidate after the approved overview/ratings pass. The approved local changes remain the incumbent; no deployment is planned for this candidate.

Hypothesis: removing duplicate scope surfaces and compounded group separators makes complete ability text easier to reach and scan without changing its reading measure or paragraph rhythm.

Scope: reader-only markup for scoped feedback, plus reader feedback and ability-group spacing selectors. Keep the existing search/chapter handlers and complete rendering unchanged. Do not alter the prior overview, ratings or synergy candidates.
